# Design System: Earth's Nature Heaven (Open Design Craft)

An organic, heavenly portfolio aesthetic heavily inspired by Earth's natural sanctuaries, lush canopy meadows, and ethereal sky paradises, blended with modern high-craft interaction engineering.

---

## 1. Philosophical Grounding: 80% Proven + 20% Nature Heaven Soul

- **80% Proven Engineering Patterns:**
  - Clear semantic hierarchy (Hero → Vital Stats → Story Carousel → Career Growth Timeline → Skills Glade → High-Impact Projects → Open Source Garden → Teammate Echoes → Contact Soil).
  - Uncompromising contrast (WCAG AAA for all essential reading text, minimum 7:1 against sunlit sky and parchment surfaces).
  - Fluid responsive layouts, full keyboard navigation, accessible motion toggles (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`).

- **20% Distinctive Nature Heaven Soul:**
  - **Atmospheric Celestial Sky & Meadow Hero:** Seamless video meadow scene (`hero-scene.webm`) with live parallax clouds (`clouds-left.png`, `clouds-right.png`) drifting over sunlit skies.
  - **Living Pollen & Firefly Particle Canvas:** Organic spores, dandelion fluff, and glowing golden pollen floating gently on a breeze, dispersing with smooth fluid inertia when touched by the cursor.
  - **Botanical Terrarium Cards:** Frosted greenhouse glass surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` / `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`) with morning dew borders (`rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.16)`), 3D leaf tilt, and sunbeam light refraction.
  - **Editorial Botanical Typography:** High-contrast editorial serif (*Fraunces*) paired with crisp *Plus Jakarta Sans* and nature-rooted telemetry monospaced type (*IBM Plex Mono*).
  - **Living Ecosystem Architecture Widget:** Instead of cold tech terminals, an interactive living botanical ecosystem showcasing iOS architecture, scale metrics, and open-source contributions.
  - **Grassland Horizon Footer:** Lush pixel meadow rim (`footer-bg.webp`) grounding the page.

## 3. Typography Architecture

- **Display Headline:** `'Fraunces', Georgia, serif`
  - Editorial, literary, natural curves.
  - Weight: 600 with soft italic accents for poetic words (`move & bloom`, `engineering gets done`).
- **Body & Interface:** `'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, sans-serif`
  - Crystal clear, open letterforms, comfortable 1.65 line height.
- **Botanical Telemetry & Tags:** `'IBM Plex Mono', monospace`
  - Rooted, precise, monospaced tags for technologies, stats, and engineering metrics.

---

## 4. Anti-AI-Slop Strict Guarantees

1. **NO Generic Dark Obsidian / Cyberpunk Hacker Terminal:** Replaced with a living, sunlit, heavenly garden and botanical terrarium.
2. **NO Two-Stop Purple-to-Indigo Gradients:** Replaced with soft sunbeams, atmospheric canopy gradients, and lush emerald leaves.
3. **NO Emoji Feature Icons:** Monoline 1.6px botanical SVGs and crisp typography badges.
4. **NO Hard Borders or Clunky Left Lines:** Replaced with frosted dew glass, soft botanical shadows, and subtle leaf vein borders.
5. **Real Concrete Metrics Only:** Navi, MakeMyTrip, Nuclei, Apple, Google, AppLovin, 99.9% crash-free, 50M+ users.
